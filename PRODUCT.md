# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static pages, fast on cheap phones, good for Google), small client scripts for enrolment, progress, quizzes and the checkout, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Same stack as the other demo sites.

## Users

- **Real audience: computer training institutes, freelancing trainers, coding bootcamps and teachers in Pakistan** whom the software house shows this demo to, usually on a phone over WhatsApp. They judge whether their own courses would look this good online, whether students could enrol and pay without calling them, and whether students would actually finish.
- **Story audience: young people in Pakistan learning computer and IT skills to get a job or earn online**: students after matric or intermediate, graduates looking for a first job, people who want to freelance on Fiverr and Upwork. Mostly on a phone, often on mobile data, many unsure whether they are "technical enough", comparing free and paid courses and asking "will this get me work?".

## Product Purpose

A demonstration learning site for a fictional online IT school, built by the software house to show what a course website from them looks like and does: a course catalogue people want to start, clear course pages with the full syllabus, free courses that open at once, paid courses with a checkout that offers the payment methods Pakistani students use, a lesson player that remembers progress, quizzes and a certificate at the end. Success: an institute owner asks for one like it.

## Positioning

An institute's own learning site instead of WhatsApp groups and YouTube playlists: every course shows its syllabus, hours, level and price up front, free courses start in one tap, paid courses take JazzCash, Easypaisa or card, and students see their progress and earn a certificate, so the institute sells and teaches while the owner sleeps.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Facebook and Instagram ads, WhatsApp shares and Google. Pakistani IT training: MS Office and computer basics for office jobs, web development, Python, graphic design, digital marketing and freelancing, WordPress. Prices in PKR, some courses free to build trust, certificates matter for job applications, many learners study at night on a phone and resume where they stopped.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything is a demo: the school, courses, instructors, prices, student numbers and reviews are made up and labelled as a demo where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- No video files: lessons show the lesson notes, key points and a placeholder where the video would play, labelled as a demo.
- Enrolment, progress, quiz answers and certificates live in the browser on this device only. Nothing is sent to a server.
- Checkout is simulated: no payment is taken, and there are no card, wallet, PIN or CNIC fields at all. JazzCash, Easypaisa and card are plain demo steps that say so.
- No real phone or WhatsApp numbers. Example numbers read 03XX XXXXXXX.
- English copy, with Urdu where it helps (a few labels, encouragement).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real school, courses, instructors or reviews. The catalogue and lessons are written for the demo.

## Product Principles

1. Show the whole course before asking for money: syllabus, hours, level, what you will be able to do.
2. Start in one tap: free courses and free preview lessons open without an account.
3. Pay the Pakistani way: JazzCash, Easypaisa and card, clear and equal, and honest that this demo charges nothing.
4. Finishing is the product: progress that is always visible, the next lesson one tap away, a certificate worth showing.
5. Honest demo: nobody should think they paid or earned a real certificate.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual direction is the category standard, chosen by the user on 2026-10-26: a clean, familiar course site in the class of Udemy and Coursera (white pages, course cards with covers, one brand colour, a dark course header band, a sticky enrol card). Their craft level is the bar. Played straight, with no quirky theme smuggled in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable type on a small phone, strong contrast, works on slow connections with no heavy media, keyboard and screen reader friendly lesson player and quizzes, plain English a matric student can follow.
